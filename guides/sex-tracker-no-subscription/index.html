Sex tracker with no subscription: pay once or subscribe?

By the team at OMS Group, makers of Petaly · Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Most trackers in this category are free to download and ask for money later. If you're looking for a sex tracker with no subscription, the way an app asks tells you a lot about how it's built, and about what happens to your history if you stop paying.

When a subscription makes sense, and when it doesn't

A subscription pays for something that keeps costing the developer money: servers, sync, storage, support for an account system. If an app runs on a cloud, a recurring fee is an honest model.

A tracker that keeps your entries on your phone has no such running cost. When one still charges monthly, it's fair to ask what the fee is for. Sometimes the answer is new features and ongoing development, which is a legitimate reason. Sometimes it's access to your own history, which isn't.

Three questions to ask before you pay

1. What happens to my entries if I stop paying?

Some apps lock features you already used, or stop letting you view or export your own history. Look for a plain answer on the store page. If it isn't there, assume the worst.

2. What am I actually unlocking?

A good paywall unlocks more (customization, insights, extra tools), not your own data. If export or backup sits behind the paywall, you're renting access to your own records.

3. Does the price match what the app costs to run?

An app with no servers and no accounts has no hosting bill to cover. A one-time unlock is the pricing model that matches that architecture.

What you keep if you never pay

With Petaly, the whole tracker is free: unlimited entries, your full history, the calendar, basic stats, app lock and a free export. Nothing you've logged is ever hidden behind payment.

How Petaly does it

There are no renewals, no account and nothing to cancel. Your entries live on your device, and there are no Petaly servers to pay for, so there is nothing to subscribe to. The short version

  • A subscription covers an ongoing cost to the developer. Ask what the cost is.
  • Your history should never be what you're renting.
  • A one-time unlock is the pricing model that fits an app with no servers.
